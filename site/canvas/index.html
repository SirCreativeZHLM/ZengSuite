ZengCanvas

The whiteboard you don't pay for.

An infinite canvas for ideas, sketches and diagrams, with a Tidy button for the mess.

Download ZengCanvasAll apps

Signature feature

Tidy

Select a pile of objects and pick a mode: Grid, Row, Column, Radial, Tree, Mind map, Colours or Rhythm. Hovering a mode previews it live, with everything gliding there on a spring and gliding back if you move away. Click to commit as one undo step. Related: Rough to neat turns wobbly hand-drawn rectangles, ellipses and lines into crisp shapes.

What it does

  • Infinite and smooth

    Pan, pinch and zoom with momentum, a minimap, and rendering that only repaints what moves.

  • Draw naturally

    Pen, marker, highlighter, pencil, erasers and lasso. Hold the pen still to snap a wobbly shape.

  • Lots of objects

    26 shapes, text boxes, notes, tables, frames, link cards, images, stamps and connectors that follow their shapes.

  • Boards and presenting

    Several boards per file, and present your frames one by one with a laser pointer.

  • Export anything

    PNG or JPEG at 1 to 4 times size, real vector SVG, PDF through the print dialog, or copy as PNG.

Formats

  • .zfree
  • .png
  • .jpg
  • .svg
  • .pdf export
  • .json

The first one is the native, open format. Others are read and written as listed.

Keyboard

  • Command paletteCtrlK
  • Tidy as gridCtrlAltT
  • Rough to neatCtrlAltR
  • PanSpace

Good to know

  • PDF goes through the system print dialog rather than being written directly.
  • Groups are one level deep. Tidy treats frames as fixed. Rough to neat reads upright shapes only and leaves curves alone.
  • SVG import ignores gradients, filters, masks and CSS classes. Table cells hold plain text.
  • Only tested in a desktop browser engine and the macOS web view so far.
