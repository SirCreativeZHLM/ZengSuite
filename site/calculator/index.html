What it does

  • Three modes

    Basic for the till, scientific for the exam, programmer for hex, binary and bit work.

  • A tape of everything you did

    Scroll back, click a result and reuse it.

  • Keyboard first

    Type the whole sum and press Enter; no mouse needed.

  • Copy exactly

    Results copy at full precision, not what fits on the display.

Formats
