Signature feature

Clean Scan

Photograph a piece of paper and get a clean PDF page. Look finds the page edges (you can drag the corners, with a magnifier), corrects the perspective and snaps to a real paper size, straightens the page, removes shadows and offers Colour, Paper white, Grey and Ink black looks. Batch several pages, and optionally add a searchable text layer with offline OCR (English).

What it does

  • PDFs that stay fast

    Only visible pages are rendered, so a 260-page file is fine. Thumbnails, outline, search with a results list, and password-protected files.

  • Mark up and sign

    Highlight, underline, strike, notes, text boxes, shapes, pen, drawn or typed signature, redaction and form filling. Markup is saved as real PDF annotations that other viewers show.

  • Page tools

    Reorder, rotate, delete, insert, extract, merge, split by range, crop, compress, and export pages as PNG or JPEG.

  • An image viewer too

    Slideshow, contact sheet, rotate, crop, adjust exposure and colour, resize and export, EXIF panel, and combine images into a PDF.

Good to know

  • Redaction burns the whole page into a picture, so all text, links and fields on that page are lost.
  • Reordering, deleting or inserting pages rebuilds the file: bookmarks and interactive form structure of the original are not carried over. Password-protected PDFs can be read and marked up, but saving offers a flattened copy.
  • Markup is editable again only in files last saved by Look. OCR is English only and takes a few seconds per page.
  • TIFF depends on the system web view (not available on Windows). It is the biggest app, about 14 MB, because it includes a PDF engine and OCR.
