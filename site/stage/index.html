Signature feature

Remix

Remix lays the current slide out again in six designed variants: Hero, Split, Big Number, Grid, Quote and Timeline. It finds your title, bullets, images, the number worth shouting about and any quote, then arranges them with the deck's own fonts and colours. Preview the variants in a spring-driven carousel, apply one, or apply one to the whole deck as a single undo step.

What it does

  • A proper editor

    Slide navigator with live thumbnails, rulers, guides, smart snapping, grouping, align and distribute, and 120 steps of undo.

  • Lots to put on a slide

    Rich text, 94 shapes, connectors that stay attached, images with cropping and masks, tables, charts with a data grid, audio and video.

  • Themes, transitions and builds

    14 original themes with editable palettes, ten transitions including Magic Move, and object builds with a timeline.

  • Presenting

    Full-screen play with laser pointer and slide overview, presenter display with next slide, notes and timer, and Rehearse to record timings.

  • Many ways out

    Save .pptx, PDF (through the print dialog), PNG or JPEG, or one self-contained HTML presentation.

Formats

Good to know

  • Animations and builds are not written to .pptx (they stay in .zkey), and cube, flip, zoom and Magic Move export as a fade. Audio and video are not embedded in .pptx.
  • .pptx import skips SmartArt, OLE objects, EMF and WMF images and slide animations.
  • The presenter display is an overlay in the same window, not a second-screen window. There is no video export.
  • .pptx output was checked by parsing it and round-tripping it through the importer, not in another presentation app.
