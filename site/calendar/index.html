Signature feature

Time Balance

For the week or month you are looking at, see your time by calendar and category, a meeting-load meter and a free-time ribbon with your longest open window marked. A thin green strip beside each working day shows free windows: drag along it to protect that time as a focus block. Focus blocks are ordinary events, so they export, and quick add and Find Free Time respect them.

What it does

  • Every view

    Day, work week, week, multi-day, month, year and list, with a current-time line and drag to create, move or resize.

  • Quick add in plain language

    Type Lunch with Sam tomorrow 1pm for 1h at Cafe, or Standup every weekday 9:30am.

  • Serious repeats and time zones

    Full RFC 5545 repeat rules, edit one, this and future, or all occurrences, and per-event time zones.

  • Find free time and holidays

    Search, Find Free Time, and holiday calendars for ten countries computed by rule for any year.

  • Yours to move

    Standard .ics import and export, and CSV import and export of spreadsheet-style files.

Good to know

  • Invitations cannot be sent or answered. Attendees are stored and exported only.
  • Alerts are in-app banners and only fire while the app is running. Missed ones are listed at the next launch.
  • Subscribing to a web calendar needs a network, which this app never uses. Subscribe from File mirrors a local .ics instead and refreshes only when you ask.
  • Holidays are national dates only. CSV has no recurrence and only English headers are read.
