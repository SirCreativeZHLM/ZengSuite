What it does

  • Real pages, not an endless scroll

    Page layout with headers, footers, page numbers, columns and footnotes, so what you see is what prints.

  • Styles that do the work

    Paragraph and character styles, an outline sidebar and an automatic table of contents.

  • Tables, images and shapes

    Drop them in, drag them where you want, wrap text around them.

  • Word files in, Word files out

    Open and save .docx, export PDF, print. Nobody has to know what you wrote it in.
