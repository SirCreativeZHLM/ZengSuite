ZengCalc

The calculator that shows its work.

Basic, scientific, programmer, converter, dates, graphing and scratch notes, with Show Work for every result.

Download ZengCalcAll apps

Signature feature

Show Work

Every result can explain itself, in any mode. See the steps in evaluation order with a plain reason for each, the same evaluation as a tree, and a what-if view: click a number in the expression, or drag it, and the result, the changed steps and the percentage change update live. In Scratch mode a slider re-evaluates every line that depends on a variable. Copy the working as text or as an image card.

What it does

  • Exact arithmetic

    Decimal maths that gives 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3, division and roots to 34 digits, and percent that works the way people mean it.

  • Seven modes

    Basic, Scientific, Programmer, Converter, Date Calculation, Graphing and Scratch.

  • Programmer and Converter

    64, 32, 16 and 8-bit words with a clickable bit grid and linked hex, decimal, octal and binary. Thirteen kinds of unit, with exchange rates you type in.

  • Graphing and Scratch

    Up to six functions with sliders, trace, zeros and intersections. Scratch lines like rent = 1200 and 5 km in miles give live answers.

  • Paper tape history

    A persistent tape you can scroll back through.

Formats

Nothing to open or save. It is a calculator.

Keyboard

  • Show WorkCtrlShiftK
  • Switch modeCtrl1 to 7
  • Copy resultCtrlC
  • ClearEsc

Good to know

  • Trigonometry, logarithms and exponentials use double precision, about 15 digits.
  • Graphing is y = f(x) only. Scratch units do not support compound units beyond built-ins like km/h, and multiplying two quantities is an error.
  • Currency rates are whatever you type in. Nothing is fetched, because the app never uses the network.
