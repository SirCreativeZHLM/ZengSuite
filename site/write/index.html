Signature feature

Focus + Readability Lens

Focus mode dims everything except the paragraph or sentence you are writing, with optional typewriter scrolling, and the interface steps out of the way. The Lens panel shows reading and speaking time, reading-ease and grade scores, long sentences, possible passive voice, doubled and overused words (click one to highlight every use) and a word count per heading. It all runs on your computer, and the checks are tuned for English.

What it does

  • Real pages, visible while you type

    Text flows across separate sheets, with widow and orphan control. Print and PDF output come from the same layout, so they match the screen.

  • Page setup that goes the distance

    Letter, A4, Legal, A5 or custom sizes, margins, one to three columns, headers and footers with page-number fields, page and column breaks.

  • Styles, lists, tables and objects

    Paragraph styles you can change, outline numbering, checklists, tables, images with text wrapping, shapes, footnotes, an automatic table of contents and equations.

  • Review tools

    Comments with replies, track changes, find and replace with regular expressions, and live word and page counts.

  • Templates and safety

    New from template: Letter, Resume, Report, Newsletter, Meeting Notes. Drafts autosave and you are offered recovery after a crash.

Good to know

  • The .docx files were checked by unzipping, parsing every part and reading them with an independent library, but not opened in a commercial word processor. Keep the original of anything important.
  • PDF export uses the system print dialog: choose Save as PDF.
  • Footnotes are placed at the end of the document. In multi-column mode paragraphs move between columns whole. Table header rows do not repeat on later pages.
  • Track changes covers typed text and deletions, not formatting changes or pasted content. There are no free-floating text boxes.
