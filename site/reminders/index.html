What it does

  • Lists and smart lists

    Today, Scheduled and Flagged build themselves from your reminders.

  • Due dates, times and repeats

    Daily, weekly, monthly, whatever your life needs.

  • Subtasks

    Break a big thing into small things and tick them off.

  • Offline and private

    Nothing is synced anywhere. It is a file on your computer.
