Signature feature

Today's Three

An optional daily ritual. Todo scores your open tasks from due date, priority, flag, age and how often you have postponed them, suggests candidates, and you drag up to three into numbered slots. The Today view then shows your three, with a local-only tally and streak. Press P to postpone with a reason, and a task postponed three times is marked Stuck and offered a split into subtasks.

What it does

  • Smart lists and your own

    Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged, Completed and No Date with live counts, plus coloured lists, groups, templates and tags.

  • Quick add that parses as you type

    Pay rent every 1st at 9am !!! #home in Work sets the date, repeat, priority, tag and list.

  • Real tasks

    Due times, repeats, early alerts, priorities, flags, notes, links, image attachments and subtasks with progress.

  • Satisfying to finish

    Spring-animated checkboxes with undo, and 200 levels of undo and redo.

  • Safe data

    Written within 300 ms, with two rotating slots and a daily backup.

Formats

Good to know

  • Alerts only fire while the app is running. There is no background service and no system notifications.
  • Time zones are not modelled: .ics times with a time zone are read as local wall-clock time, and exports use floating local times.
  • Subtasks are one level deep. Natural-language parsing is English only, and an ambiguous time such as at 5 is guessed.
