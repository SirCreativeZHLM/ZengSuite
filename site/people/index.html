Signature feature

Relationship rhythm

Give a contact a cadence such as every two months, and People keeps a timeline of catch-ups and notes. The Catch up view lists who is due, most overdue first, with one-tap email and call. Birthday and anniversary nudges are built in, and the People map shows contacts as a constellation clustered by company or group, sized by how recently you spoke.

What it does

  • Proper contact cards

    Photos with crop, structured addresses, birthdays, related names, social profiles, notes, custom labels and phonetic names.

  • Groups and smart groups

    Rules such as is due, company contains or birthday this month, plus favourites and search across everything.

  • Tidy up

    Duplicate finder with per-field merge, and clean-up of empty or invalid cards.

  • Print

    Lists, cards, envelopes and address labels.

  • Open formats

    Import and export vCard 2.1, 3.0 and 4.0 and CSV with a mapping dialog, plus backups.

Formats

  • .vcf
  • .csv
  • .zcontacts backup

The first one is the native, open format. Others are read and written as listed.

Keyboard

  • New contactCtrlN
  • Log a catch-upL
  • FindF
  • Catch up viewCtrl4

Good to know

  • There is no sync and no account. Your contacts live on this computer.
  • Remote photo URLs in vCards are kept as text and not fetched. Large photos are re-encoded to 320 px.
  • The People map draws at most 700 people.
