What it does

  • PDFs that open instantly

    Thumbnails, continuous scroll, search inside the document, zoom that stays sharp.

  • Mark up and sign

    Highlight, underline, draw, add text and place your signature.

    Reorder, rotate and delete pages, and merge several PDFs into one.
