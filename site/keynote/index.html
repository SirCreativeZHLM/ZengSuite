What it does

  • Slide navigator and layouts

    Reorder by dragging, pick a layout, keep a consistent look across the deck.

  • Transitions and builds

    Smooth slide transitions and object animations that run at your display's refresh rate.

  • Presenter view

    Current slide, next slide, your notes and a timer on your screen while the audience sees the deck.

  • PowerPoint in, PowerPoint out

    Open and save .pptx, export PDF for handouts.

Formats

  • .zkey
  • .pptx

The first one is what it reads and writes best.
