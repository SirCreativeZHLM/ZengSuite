# ZengSuite — product

**Register:** product (Operate mode for the apps) + brand (Persuade mode for the zengbot.com/suite website).

ZengSuite is a free, open-source office and creativity suite for macOS, Windows and Linux: the full set of Apple's Mac apps (Pages, Numbers, Keynote, Preview, Notes, Freeform, Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Calculator, TextEdit) built to the standard of Microsoft 365, with none of the subscription. Tagline on the site: **F*ck Microslop.**

## Users
Anyone who needs to write, calculate, present and organise without paying monthly, signing in, or being tracked: students, families, small businesses, Linux users who were never offered Pages, Mac people who miss it on a Windows work PC.

## Principles
- Free means free: no accounts, no ads, no telemetry, no paywalled features, MIT licensed.
- Native, not Electron: Rust + the OS web view; each app is its own small binary with its own download.
- The apps are only the apps. All marketing lives on the website, none in the software.
- Apple-grade craft: fast response, direct manipulation, interruptible springs, restraint.
- Open formats first, with real import/export for .docx .xlsx .pptx .pdf .ics .vcf .csv.

## Anti-references
SaaS landing-page clichés (gradient hero + three feature cards + pricing table), enterprise grey, cloud upsells inside the app, anything that looks like it is selling.

## Assumptions (no interview was possible — user said "build it now")
Brand voice is blunt, funny, proud of being free. Platforms: macOS arm64/x64, Windows x64, Linux x64. Site lives at zengbot.com/suite, downloads on GitHub Releases at SirCreativeZHLM/ZengSuite.
